## What it is

A RAG-powered web platform where users converse with AI models trained on
culturally contextualised datasets, so responses stay fair and polite across
diverse social contexts.

## Highlights

- **Culturally grounded RAG pipeline** — a rigidly constrained LLM setup using
  retrieval-augmented generation, keeping responses grounded in validated,
  culturally appropriate knowledge bases.
- **Bring-your-own integration** — personal API key management lets users
  generate and manage keys to plug the culturally aware models into their own
  apps and workflows.
