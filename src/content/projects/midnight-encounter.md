## What it is

A horror game built in Unity for a game development course (WIG3005) at
Universiti Malaya. Something is out there at midnight, and you get to meet it.

## Highlights

- **Full Unity pipeline** — scenes, scripting, lighting, and builds handled
  end to end in C#.
- **Atmosphere on a deadline** — horror pacing and tension designed within a
  single course semester.
