## What it is

A ground-up remaster of the corporate website for Vision One Sdn. Bhd., an
oil & gas services company operating since 1997.

## Highlights

- **Full corporate presence** — services, projects, and contact flows for a
  company whose work spans advanced inspection, non-destructive testing, and
  maintenance in the energy sector.
- **Hand-built front end** — plain HTML/CSS/JS, no framework overhead, fast
  on the connections site visitors actually have.
