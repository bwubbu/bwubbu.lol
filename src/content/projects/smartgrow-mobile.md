## What it is

The mobile front-end for SmartGrow, a student-built IoT plant-management
system. It brings the desktop dashboard to phones as a responsive, PWA-style
interface for monitoring and controlling grow hardware on the go.

## Highlights

- **Mobile-first dashboard** — the SmartGrow readouts and controls, reworked
  for small screens and touch.
- **PWA delivery** — installable, responsive web tech so growers can check on
  their plants without a desktop.
