## What it is

The online home of the NeCopy Project — an arts, fashion and lifestyle brand.
Browse the collection, shop the merch, live the ✨lifestyle✨.

## Highlights

- **Full storefront** — product browsing, inquiries, and returns flow for a
  real brand shipping real things.
- **Brand-first design** — the site carries the label's identity end to end,
  from typography to the tone of every page.
