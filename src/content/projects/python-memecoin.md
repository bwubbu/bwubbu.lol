## What it is

A data-driven look at memecoins — Dogecoin, Shiba Inu, Pepe and friends —
built in Python and Jupyter. It walks from an introduction to memecoin culture
through the data behind their volatile, community-driven markets.

## Highlights

- **From meme to market** — connects internet culture and influencer hype to
  real price volatility and retail participation.
- **Notebook-driven** — analysis and findings laid out in Jupyter, Python
  doing the heavy lifting.
